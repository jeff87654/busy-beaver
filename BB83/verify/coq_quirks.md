# Coq quirks in the BB83 development

## 1. Cut the blank-tape run into chunks

`BB83_start.v` proves 152 steps with 10-step chunks, generated by
`../tools/gen_start_chunks.py`.  A single `execute` over the whole run
re-normalises the accumulated proof term at every step and is cubic.

The final equality needs a little stream normalisation:

    unfold cfg_152, Bcfg. simpl.
    rewrite <- (const_unfold sym S0).
    reflexivity.

## 2. Keep the parameter chain opaque

`m1`…`m5`, `next`, `round` and `mfin` are opaque.  In `BB83_run.v`, writing
`change (... m1 ...)` while `m1 := Nat.iter 3 round 6` was transparent made Coq
try to normalise an Ackermann-level numeral and hang.  Use the `_eq` lemmas and
`rewrite`, not conversion.

`BB83_score.v` temporarily makes `next` transparent only to expose its defining
`Nat.iter (2*m+2) round 6`; `round` itself stays opaque.

## 3. The reset rules are different from BB73

BB83 reuses BB73's `B_rule` and `rule2`, but its tail rules are:

    B(1,0,m) 0 0 r -> B(2m+3,1,0) 0 r
    B(1,0,m) 0 1 r -> B(2,1,0) 0^(2m+3) r
    B(1,0,m) 0 2 r -> halt

The old BB73 `rule3` and `D_left_2s` do not apply.  `rule3b` needs the
additional sweep `D_left_1s` and the helper `lpow_pred2_eq` to turn
`0 >> [0]^^(pred(pred j)) *> r` into `[0]^^(j-1) *> r`.

## 4. Bcfg still prepends the `1^n` block

`Bcfg k n m r` has right stream `[1]^^n *> r`.  After `initial_desc`, the
visible right stream is `0 >> r`; passing an extra `0` to `tail_zeros` is a
common source of off-by-one errors.

## 5. Arithmetic and stream-normalisation tactics

* `lia` knows `<=`, `<` and `=` but not `>=`, and cannot compare applications
  of opaque functions except as atoms.  Prove equalities of arguments first.
* Natural numerals in `Bcfg`/`round` arguments sometimes need `%nat`, because
  `0`, `1`, `2` are notation for symbols in `sym_scope`.
* Avoid `simpl` on closed terms containing `Nat.iter ... f_omega1 ...`: it
  unfolds `f_omega1` and tries to normalise enormous arguments.  Use `change`
  with the explicit unfoldings instead.
* The clean build writes `compile_clean.log` and `probe.log`; the latter must
  contain 9 `Closed under the global context` lines and no `Axioms:` line.
