# Coq quirks in the BB73 development

## 1. Cut the blank-tape run into chunks

`BB73_start.v` proves 255 steps with 26 chunk lemmas of 10 steps (and a final
5-step chunk).  A single `execute` over 255 steps re-normalises the whole
accumulated proof term at every step (cubic in the step count) and exhausts
coqc's memory.  The file was generated by `../tools/gen_start_chunks.py`.

## 2. Seal `mfin`, and make `round` and `f_omega` opaque

`mfin = round^12(1)` is a closed natural number with Ackermann-level size.  It
is stored as `proj1_sig mfin_spec`, with `Opaque mfin` immediately after the
definition.  `round` and `f_omega` are transparent while their closed forms
are proved, then made opaque (`Opaque round f_omega.`) before the iteration
argument.  Otherwise `reflexivity` or `simpl` on a goal containing
`Nat.iter 1 round 1` tries to normalise an astronomical natural number and
does not terminate in practice.

The rule results are abstracted in the same way as BB34's `g15`: `B_rule_gen`
takes the huge number as a parameter and a hypothesis, rather than forcing Coq
to compare two syntactic forms of the same number.

## 3. `desc` is indexed by the number of rule-2 applications

One round from `B(1,0,m) 0 1 tail` is

    round m = desc (2m+2) (g (2m+2) 0),

not `desc (2m+3) 0`.  The initial 1 is consumed by `g_(2m+2)`, and then the
`2m+2` rule-2 descents apply `g_(2m+2)`, `g_(2m+1)`, ..., `g_1` once more.
Getting this index wrong changes the closed form by one Knuth level.

## 4. Bcfg prepends the `1^n` block to `r`

`Bcfg k n m r` has right stream `[1]^^n *> r`.  Thus the configuration after
`rule3` is `Bcfg (2m+3) 1 0 (0 >> r)`, whose *visible* right stream begins with
`1 >> 0 >> r`.  Passing `1 >> 0 >> r` as the `r` argument of `B_rule_gen`
duplicates the leading 1; the correct tail parameter is `0 >> r`.

## 5. Arithmetic and stream-normalisation tactics

* `lia` knows `<=`, `<` and `=` but not `>=`, and cannot compare applications
  of opaque functions (`knuth`, `round`) except as atoms.  Prove equalities of
  the arguments first, then rewrite.
* `norm_hd` must be defined after `hd_lpow_gen` and `tl_lpow_gen` (from
  `BB73_rules.v`), otherwise Coq cannot find those lemmas.
* `simpl` unfolds fixpoints transitively; avoid it on terms containing `g`,
  `desc`, `round` or large `Nat.iter`s.  Prefer explicit defining equations
  and `rewrite`.
* `app_length` is deprecated in Coq 8.20; use `length_app`.
